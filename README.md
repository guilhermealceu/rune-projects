## About Rune Projects

**Rune Projects** is an independent digital ecosystem created by **Guilherme Dill**, focused on artificial intelligence, music, storytelling, productivity, anime-inspired experiences and modern web platforms.

The ecosystem brings together multiple digital products under one creative and technological identity, combining AI-powered tools, independent creator platforms, immersive web experiences and original virtual characters.

**Official Website:** https://www.runeprojects.com.br  
**Nixie Official Page:** https://www.runeprojects.com.br/nixie  

---

## The Rune Universe

Rune Projects is more than a collection of websites.

It is a growing creative technology ecosystem where artificial intelligence, music, storytelling, productivity and digital identity coexist.

The long-term vision is to build a connected universe of platforms where users can create, publish, listen, organize, explore and interact through modern web experiences.

---

## Nixie

**Nixie** is the official virtual character of the Rune Projects ecosystem.

She represents the creative, technological and imaginative identity behind the Rune universe — blending artificial intelligence, anime-inspired storytelling, digital culture, music and futuristic web experiences.

Nixie is not simply a mascot.  
She is an evolving original virtual character designed to exist across multiple Rune Projects platforms, stories, visual experiences and creative products.
